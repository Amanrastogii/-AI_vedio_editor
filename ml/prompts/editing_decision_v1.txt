## Editing Principles
- First 3 seconds must be IMMEDIATELY engaging — no slow opens
- Cut on action (cut while something is happening, not before or after)
- Match cut on similar motion/direction between clips
- Use jump cuts for energy, dissolves for emotional moments
- A single dialogue clip should be max 15 seconds before a cutaway
- End on an emotionally resonant frame, not a random moment
- Zoom-ins should be VERY subtle (1.05-1.2x max) and motion-smoothed
- Remove: stumbled words, long pauses > 0.8s, looking off-camera for > 1s
